**NEON HARBOR — 6 story missions, 4-star NHPD dispatch, helicopter & roadblocks, weapon wheel, on-foot shootouts, garage upgrades, respect, saves, radio.**

A neon-noir open-world crime game in the browser. You are **Rook**, a driver-for-hire climbing the harbor underworld of a coastal city policed by the **NHPD** (Neon Harbor Police Department). Golden-hour sun, pink-purple skies, five districts — The Piers, Neon Mile, Downtown, Palm Row, Dockside — grid streets full of traffic, pedestrians, palms and billboards.

## How to play

- **Arrow keys / WASD** — drive or walk · **Shift** nitro · **Space** brake
- **E** — enter / steal a car, step out, enter the garage
- **Mouse + Click** — aim and shoot on foot · **Q / 1–5** — weapon wheel (fists, pistol, SMG)
- **F** — punch · **R** — change radio station (Harbor FM 98.1 / Baywave 104.7)
- **M** — map (missions DONE / NEXT / LOCKED + career stats) · **P / Esc** — pause

## What's inside

- **6 story missions** with prices, grades (S/A/B/C) and pay: steal a car, race the harbor loop against the clock, and a dockside shootout against Marrow's crew — plus three locked jobs to chase.
- **NHPD dispatch panel** — live units with callsigns, distances and statuses (PURSUIT / RAMMING / SEARCHING / SPOTLIGHT / ROADBLOCK), a police helicopter with a spotlight cone at 3+ stars, and roadblocks across the road ahead.
- **Wanted stars with a search phase** — hide and the heat cools; get caught on foot and it's BUSTED, go down in a fight and it's WASTED.
- **On-foot firearms** — pistol and SMG with ammo, crosshair, muzzle flash, armed enemies with red markers and a down counter, a boss with dialog.
- **Grease's Spray & Tune garage** — repair, respray, engine tunes and armour plating with car stat bars.
- **Respect ranks** — ROOKIE → HUSTLER → WHEELMAN → ENFORCER → KINGPIN, plus 10 harbor coins to collect, day/time clock, vehicle card, and saves that persist across sessions.
- **Named characters with dialog** — Vera the fixer, Detective Dial on NHPD dispatch, Marrow the dockside boss, Grease the garage owner.

## Tech

Three.js (vendored locally, no network at runtime), plain ES modules, no build step. Serve with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. Screenshot modes via `?shot=cover|shootout|garage|map|title`.
